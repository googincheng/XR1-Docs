# XRecer XR1 User Manual

- **Version:** V0.6.1
- **Date:** May 2026

This manual covers the XR1 hardware, Quick Start, Signal Chain, Live View, audio and MIDI routing, and effect parameters.

On the touchscreen, tap, tap and hold, or double-tap. On a footswitch, press or press and hold. Turn a knob to adjust a value.
