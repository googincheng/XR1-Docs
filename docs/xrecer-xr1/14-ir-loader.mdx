# IR Loader

An impulse response (IR) simulates how sound travels, reflects, and builds up in a physical space. Imagine a room with concrete walls versus one with wood walls, or a 10 m² room versus a 100 m² room — what you hear is completely different. IR helps you recreate the feel of that real space. You can use XRecer's built-in space IRs, or import IRs you made yourself or from third parties, to make your sound more realistic.

## Load an IR File

<ManualImage className="manual-inline-icon" src="/img/manual/image89.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="64"/> 

1.  Add an IR Loader to the Signal Chain.

2.  Tap the IR Loader to access its parameters, then browse and select an IR file.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image90.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

3.  Parameters:

    -   Bypass: turns this IR on or off

    -   IR File: selects the IR file

    -   Phase Flip: reverses the polarity of the IR

    -   LPF: low-pass filter cutoff

    -   HPF: high-pass filter cutoff

    -   Delay: time before the IR response starts

    -   Pan: left/right balance
