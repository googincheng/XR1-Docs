# Quick Start

## 1. Connect an Instrument or Microphone

Connect your instrument to any INPUT jack.

Connect a microphone to MIC IN.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image3.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## 2. Connect Monitors or Headphones

Connect your studio monitors, PA, or FRFR cabinet to OUT 1/2 or OUT 3/4.

Alternatively, connect headphones to PHONES.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image4.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## 3. Select a Preset

Tap and hold **P01-01** in the upper-left corner to open the Preset list, then select a Preset.

### 

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image5.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## 4. Tune Your Instrument

Press and hold the TEMPO/TUNER footswitch to access the Tuner.

Play a string and tune your instrument. The Tuner does not change your tone.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image6.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## 5. Adjust Your Tone and Effects

Tap an effect to access its parameters, then adjust them until you get the tone you like.

Double-tap an effect to bypass or activate it.

**Tip**

Tap the parameter you want to adjust, then turn the VALUE knob in the upper-right corner.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image7.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## 6. Play

Now enjoy playing with XRecer XR1.
