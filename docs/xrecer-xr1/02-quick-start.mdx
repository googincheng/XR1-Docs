# Quick Start

## Connecting an Instrument/Microphone

Connect your instrument to any INPUT.

You can connect a microphone to MIC IN.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image3.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## Connecting an Monitor/Headphones

Connect your monitor to OUT 1/2 or OUT 3/4.

Alternatively, connect headphones to HEADPHONE OUT.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image4.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## Selecting a Preset

Tap and hold P01-01 in the upper-left corner to open the Preset list, then select a Preset.

###

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image5.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## Tuning

Press and hold the TEMPO/TUNER footswitch to open the Tuner, then tune your guitar.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image6.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## Shaping Your Tone

Tap an Effect block to open its parameter editor and shape your tone.

Double-tap an Effect block to toggle its bypass state.

> Tap the desired parameter, then turn the **VALUE** knob in the upper-right corner for precise adjustment.

<ManualImage className="manual-figure" src="/img/manual/image7.png" alt="XRecer user interface" width="600"/>

## Playing

You are now ready to play through the XRecer XR1.
